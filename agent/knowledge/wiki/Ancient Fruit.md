## Bundles

- Five gold quality Ancient Fruit are an option for [Bundle Purple.png] The Missing Bundle in the abandoned JojaMart.
- Ancient Fruit is one of the options for the [Bundle Teal.png] Rare Crops Bundle in the Pantry (Remixed).

## Recipes

Ancient Fruit is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Ancient Fruit can be used in the spool of the Sewing Machine to create the dyeable Genie Pants. It can be used in dyeing, serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in Emily's and Haley's house, 2 Willow Lane.

## Quests

Ancient Fruit is not used in any quests.

## History

- 1.0: Introduced.
- 1.1: Reduced value to 550g, down from 750g.
- 1.4: Added as option for the Missing Bundle. Can now be used in Tailoring.
- 1.5: Iridium quality is now possible when using Deluxe Fertilizer. Bugfix for stages of growth (25% non-Agriculturist, 20% and 35% Agriculturist). Now used in the remixed Rare Crops bundle.
